Lockbox Services

Managing client payments efficiently is critical for maintaining smooth financial operations. Renkim's Lockbox Services enable your organization to collect payments without handling a single piece of mail. We take care of collecting, sorting, and processing your client’s mail, depositing payments directly to your bank account on your behalf. This service streamlines your payment collection process, ensuring accuracy and timely deposits.

Flow of Operations

  1. Receiving
    1. Clients mail to PO Box
    2. Renkim collects mail
    3. Mail opened and sorted in secured environment
  2. Processing
    1. Payments scanned and deposited electronically to your bank
    2. Non-payments scanned and mailed back
  3. Reporting
    1. Daily reports confirm daily processing activities
    2. Unique IDs track each batch of records
