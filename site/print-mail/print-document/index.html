Print Document Services

Renkim has provided high quality print and mail solutions since 1982. We developed the people, processes, and technology to offer flexible print solutions. Integrated document design and composition tools, combined with our forms development department, make it possible to provide documents that appeal to the eye and maximize the use of space.

Renkim utilizes the latest Canon, Pitney-Bowes, and Ricoh print technology, providing full color ink jet and digital documents. These vibrant color print documents provide accurate, easy-to-read content. Our client services and design team collaborate closely with our partners to ensure documents meet client expectations. Utilizing on-demand color printing, partners can eliminate the need to purchase and manage customized paper inventory. Renkim has the capacity to print over 80 million statements a month.

Letter Inserting Services

Renkim operates state-of-the-art Bluecrest equipment for our partners' inserting requirements. The inserters are equipped with Bluecrest File Base integrity software to provide accountability for each document inserted. Onsite maintenance ensure uptime standards are adhered to in a fast-paced and high-speed production environment.

Renkim currently has the capacity to insert over 30 million documents per month.
