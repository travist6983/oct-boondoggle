Connect, Secure, & Engage: 3 Powerful Ways to Deliver Digital Documents via Email

Reach your customers directly in their inbox, improve customer experience, and drastically reduce your postage and paper costs with Renkim's tailored email solutions.

01. Renkim eBlast

The Approach

High engagement, zero friction.

The Benefit

Deliver your message effortlessly. Templates can include embedded document images for instant visibility and increased engagement.

How It Works

Sends customized text-based or HTML templates straight to your customer's inbox without requiring an upfront authentication or login page.

02. Renkim eNotice

The Approach

Secure, authenticated access.

The Benefit

Maximum security. Because the documents are fully interactive, you can glide your customers directly to your payment portal.

How It Works

Sends a link directing your customer to a secure authentication page on Renkim web servers. After entering their information, they are seamlessly guided to an interactive PDF or HTML version of the document via a Personalized URL (PURL).

03. Renkim PDF Attachment

The Approach

The perfect blend of security and convenience.

The Benefit

By utilizing password protection on the PDF, you seamlessly combine the ironclad security of an eNotice with the effortless convenience of an eBlast.

How It Works

Delivers a statement or critical document as a direct email attachment without requiring your customer to log into an external portal.
