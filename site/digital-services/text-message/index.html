Meet Customers on Their Screens: Next-Gen SMS & MMS Texting Solutions

Put mission-critical information directly into your customers' hands. Whether it's a quick reminder or a fully embedded statement, Renkim Texting Solutions drive faster actions and immediate engagement.

01. Renkim SMS

The Approach

Instant alerts and actionable reminders.

How It Works

Send rapid text-based alerts, bill reminders, and critical notifications straight to any mobile device.

The Benefit

Highly effective and versatile. You can easily include dynamic links back to your customer portals, company websites, or hosted documents.

02. Secure MMS Texting

The Approach

Rich media delivery with built-in security.

How It Works

Send secure, password-protected texts directly to your customer's mobile device with direct links to their statements.

The Benefit

Enhanced interactivity. Statements accessed via these secure links can contain direct pathways straight to your designated payment portals.

03. New MMS Direct Statement

The Approach

Instant, zero-click document presentation.

How It Works

Embed a clean image of the actual statement or document directly into the body of the MMS text message.

The Benefit

By eliminating the barrier of "clicks" or logins to open a document, you create a low-barrier experience that dramatically improves response rates.
