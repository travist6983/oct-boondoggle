About Our Extensive Renkim History

Renkim has a rich history of innovation and dedication. Since our inception in 1982, we've evolved from a small data processing firm to a leading provider of comprehensive data management and communication solutions, all while staying true to our roots and core values.

  1. How It Started

    Several years ago, a large, Detroit-based automotive manufacturer needed a sophisticated and secure database to handle vehicle and client information. Two programmers, Gary Perlick and Rob Swearengen, were hired to provide a solution for this complex application. In 1982, these two men officially formed Renkim Corporation, naming the company after their wives – Rena and Kim, hence RenKim. The company facilitated large scale data processing and communications management services.

  2. 100% Employee Owned

    In the late nineties, the original founders began the process of converting Renkim’s ownership from the two original founders to an ESOP (Employee Stock Ownership Plan). Still today, every single share of Renkim Corporation is owned by current Renkim employees.

  3. Adding More Industries

    Around this same time, the company began adding to their project-based service offerings with continuity-based ones catered to a variety of industries with a legal or financial background. Over the years, serving these new industries led Renkim to acquire and maintain top-of-the-line third party compliance certifications for operational procedures, physical and data security, that are still in place today.

  4. 40+ Years of Service

    Over forty years since it all began, we still service that first automobile manufacturer client, as well as handling complex data processing and storage, as well as the communications management of legal, tax, financial, and other mission-critical documents for hundreds more clients in the credit, collection, healthcare, insurance, automotive, and utility industries throughout North America.

Our Core Renkim Values

Our core values are the foundation of everything we do at Renkim. They guide our decisions, shape our company culture, and define our commitment to our clients and employees.

  • We Value Integrity

    There's no point in doing a job unless you're going to do it right. We believe in adherence to proper guidelines that allow us to do exactly that. Being honest and hard-working are values we believe aid in our integrity.

  • We Value Communication

    Our entire business is founded in our ability to help people communicate quickly, efficiently, and effectively. We carry this ethos with us through every project we work on, from beginning to end.

  • We Value Teamwork

    Without the people who support our day-to-day operations, we wouldn't be where we are. We prioritize teamwork in all that we do. That's why we're 100% employee-owned, and why we'll stay that way for years to come.

  • We Value Quality

    Just because we live in a digital age doesn't mean you can't produce quality work. Renkim strives every day to deliver results that both match the speed of the modern era, while also remaining of the highest possible quality.

  • We Value Empathy

    Part of any good business is the desire to want to understand people. We want to know where you're coming from and why, not just because it's part of our job, but because we believe that's a simple courtesy you deserve.

  • We Value Respect

    Without mutual respect, any relationship is destined to fall apart. Renkim works every day to maintain the respect of our clients and our colleagues, and we believe the results of our efforts speak for themselves.
