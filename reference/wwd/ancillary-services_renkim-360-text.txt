Renkim 360 Services

Renkim 360 is our secure document archival tool that provides a user-friendly solution for receiving, reviewing, and auditing documents in PDF format that we create and send on behalf of our clients. 

As compliance laws continue to change, PCI-DSS compliancy and data retention rules require clients to retain data and documents for up to seven years. 

Our secure online portals are branded to our clients, or even our clients’ clients, and provide 24×7 document access using client defined search mechanisms.  Fields are completely customizable.

Renkim 360 is a useful tool for customer service, accounting, legal, MAP attorneys, sales, and audits.  In addition, we give our clients complete administrative rights in their portal to allow for full control over user rights or department specificity.

By combining Renkim data processing, print and eNotice services with back-end document archival in Renkim 360, and Renkim MailTrack, clients have end-to-end access to the complete scope, from document creation to the recipient’s mailbox.

Streamline Your Document Management Today!

Efficient and secure document management is essential for compliance and operational success. Renkim 360 offers a robust solution for managing your documents, providing 24×7 access and customizable search capabilities to meet your specific needs.
