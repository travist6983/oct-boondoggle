    p: Renkim 360 is our secure document archival tool that provides a user-friendly solution for receiving, reviewing, and auditing documents in PDF format that we create and send on behalf of our clients.

    p: Our secure online portals are branded to our clients, or even our clients’ clients, and provide 24×7 document access using client defined search mechanisms. Fields are completely customizable.
    p: Renkim 360 is a useful tool for customer service, accounting, legal, MAP attorneys, sales, and audits. In addition, we give our clients complete administrative rights in their portal to allow for full control over user rights or department specificity.
